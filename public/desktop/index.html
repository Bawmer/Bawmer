      Connect your phone

      1. Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
      2. Scan the code with the phone camera.
      3. The page uses a private, self-signed certificate — choose Advanced → Continue the first time.
      4. Tap Start camera. It reconnects by itself from now on.

      Tip: add the page to your home screen to launch it like an app. Video and audio never leave your local network.

      Sync calibration

      Point the phone camera at this screen so it fills most of the picture, stop playback in your DAW, then start. The screen will flash a few times.

      What should be recorded?

      Choose the screen your DAW is on and put the DAW in full screen — everything on that screen is recorded at full resolution. Or pick just the DAW window to record it even when other windows are in front (keep it open, not minimised).
